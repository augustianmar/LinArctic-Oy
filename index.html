02Study Camp

LINARCTIC SIGNATURE PROGRAMME · FINLAND × SOUTHEAST ASIA

Study Camp
Indonesia–Lapland

LinArctic develops an international Study Camp in Finnish Lapland for schools in Indonesia and Southeast Asia. The programme combines structured learning, intercultural exchange, northern nature and coordinated study visits.

For

International schools & student groups

Designed especially for schools in Indonesia and Southeast Asia seeking a meaningful, organised learning experience in Finland.

Core destination

Rovaniemi + Luosto

Rovaniemi provides opportunities for cultural and educational visits, while Luosto brings students into Lapland’s fell landscape and northern environment.

Typical format

8 days / 7 nights in Lapland

A coordinated programme in Finland supported by pre-programme preparation and follow-up or reflection work as agreed with the school.

ONE PROGRAMME · MULTIPLE LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS

Lapland as a place to learn, connect and explore.

The Study Camp is not designed as a conventional sightseeing tour. It brings together learning, culture, international interaction and Lapland experiences.

01
Learning & study visits

Educational and institutional visits in Rovaniemi can be incorporated according to group objectives and confirmed arrangements.

02
Culture & intercultural exchange

Workshops and facilitated activities can introduce students to Finnish and international perspectives.

03
Luosto & northern nature

Hiking, nature experiences and season-appropriate learning in the fell environment.

04
Arctic culture & Lapland experiences

Potential experiences include Arktikum, reindeer culture and, when conditions allow, aurora-focused activities.
