LINARCTIC SIGNATURE PROGRAMME · FINLAND × SOUTHEAST ASIA

Study Camp
Indonesia–Lapland

Programme Available through LinArctic

LinArctic offers an international Study Camp in Finnish Lapland for schools in Indonesia and Southeast Asia. The programme is built around a combined Rovaniemi and Luosto experience, bringing together structured learning, intercultural exchange, northern nature and carefully coordinated study visits within one LinArctic programme.

For International schools & student groups

Designed especially for schools in Indonesia and Southeast Asia seeking a meaningful, organised learning experience in Finland.

Core destination Rovaniemi + Luosto

Rovaniemi provides the educational, cultural and institutional setting, while Luosto brings students directly into Lapland’s fell landscape, nature and northern environment.

Typical format 8 days / 7 nights in Lapland

A coordinated programme in Finland supported by pre-programme preparation and optional follow-up or reflection work for schools seeking a deeper learning structure.

ONE PROGRAMME · MULTIPLE LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS

Lapland experienced as a place to learn, connect and explore.

The Study Camp is not designed as a conventional sightseeing tour. LinArctic combines education, culture, international interaction and Lapland experiences into a structured programme for visiting student groups.

  1. 01
    Learning & study visits

    Educational and institutional visits in Rovaniemi can be incorporated according to the group’s objectives and confirmed programme arrangements.

  2. 02
    Culture & intercultural exchange

    Opportunities for students to encounter Finnish and international perspectives through workshops, local organisations and facilitated intercultural activities.

  3. 03
    Luosto & northern nature

    A dedicated Luosto component brings the group into the fell environment through hiking, local nature experiences and other season-appropriate programme elements.

  4. 04
    Arctic culture & Lapland experiences

    The programme can include experiences such as Arktikum, reindeer culture and, in suitable seasons and conditions, aurora-focused evening activities.

PROGRAMME GEOGRAPHY
