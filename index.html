Featured programme

IN DEVELOPMENT · FINLAND × INDONESIA

Indonesia–Lapland
Study Camp Programme

Status In development

LinArctic is developing a comprehensive international study-camp concept for students from international schools in Indonesia. The programme combines structured learning, intercultural exchange and first-hand experience of Finnish Lapland within one coordinated programme.

Target group International schools in Indonesia

Designed for student groups travelling to Finland as part of a structured educational programme.

Location Finnish Lapland

Programme development is rooted in Rovaniemi and wider Lapland, with northern nature and local institutions forming part of the learning environment.

Format Study camp & study visits

A coordinated framework for educational visits, cultural learning, programme activities and partner cooperation.

HOW LINARCTIC BUILDS IT

From concept to a working cooperation network.

LinArctic’s current responsibility is to develop the programme concept, structure and cooperation network in Finland, while coordinating dialogue with companies, educational institutions, public-sector actors and other organisations relevant to the programme.

  1. 01
    Programme design

    Building the educational and operational structure of the Study Camp.

  2. 02
    Partnership development

    Connecting the Finnish and Indonesian organisations needed to make the programme work.

  3. 03
    Study-visit coordination

    Developing visits and programme components that fit into one coherent international experience.

Why it belongs on LinArctic

The Study Camp is a concrete example of LinArctic’s wider role: turning international opportunities into structured programmes, partnerships and practical cooperation.
