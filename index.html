About LinArctic

From northern expertise to structured international cooperation.

Established in 2018, LinArctic Oy operates in international development consulting and tourism services. The company’s owners bring nearly two decades of working experience in Southeast Asia.

LinArctic develops international programmes, builds partnerships and coordinates cooperation with companies, educational institutions, public-sector actors and other organisations. Our work is grounded in practical implementation, trusted relationships and an understanding of different operating environments.

Juhani Härkönen

CEO & Chairman of the Board

Forestry and natural-resource management specialist with more than 27 years of professional experience in Finland and international assignments. His international career includes more than two decades of overseas work, including extensive experience in Indonesia and assignments elsewhere in Asia and Africa.
