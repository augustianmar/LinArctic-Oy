The people behind our work

People Behind LinArctic

Experience that bridges sectors, places and people.

01

Juhani Härkönen

CEO & Chairman of the Board

Forestry and natural-resource management specialist with more than 27 years of professional experience in Finland and international assignments. His background includes sustainable forest management, governance, climate-change mitigation and adaptation, forest carbon, REDD+, social forestry, watershed management and forestry policy.

His international career includes more than two decades of overseas work, including extensive experience in Indonesia and assignments elsewhere in Asia and Africa.

02

Mikko Ruokanen

Energy Efficiency & Building Systems

Energy-efficiency and building-systems specialist with experience dating from 2013 in energy certificates, energy calculations and energy-efficiency improvement.

His technical background includes building solutions, building services and electronics.

03

Augustian Härkönen

Programme Development & Partnership Coordinator

Specialises in international programme development, cultural event coordination and stakeholder engagement, building partnerships between companies, educational institutions, cultural organisations and public-sector actors in Finland and Southeast Asia.

His current work includes Study Camp Indonesia–Lapland, international study-visit concepts and cross-cultural initiatives. He is also a shareholder of LinArctic Oy.
